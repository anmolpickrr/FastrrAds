Good to know before you quote
General terms — apply across every service & package
  • Minimum order value is ₹10,000 — mix and match any services/tiers to reach it; the calculator's final amount reflects your full order.
  • Turnaround shown for each package is per single creative — for bulk orders or multiple creative types, overall delivery depends on quantity, creative type, package/tier, complexity, and final requirements.
  • Your turnaround timeline begins once we've received everything we need — all required assets and details, not your payment date.
  • New concepts, major creative rework, and any additions beyond what's included — extra dimensions, formats, slides, or aspect ratios — sit outside package scope across every service, and are quoted separately or as a new order.
  • AI-generated visuals can vary slightly between runs, and final creative direction may be adjusted for platform policy or technical feasibility — we always aim for the closest match to your brief.
  • GST (18%) is calculated automatically in the calculator, on the order total after any discount. Prices shown elsewhere on the page are base rates, before GST.
  • A single discount applies to the whole order, not to each creative type individually.
