Good to know before you quote
  • Timeline starts only after all required assets/details are received — not from payment date.
  • Revision limits vary by package: Reel Tier 1 and 360° Catalogue have no revisions; Tier 2–4, Static and Carousel include 1 limited-scope revision.
  • Script approval differs by AI Reel tier — Tiers 1–2 have no scripting step, Tier 3 shares 1 script for review only, Tier 4 shares 1 script + 2 hooks for approval.
  • Additional dimensions are always chargeable — every AI Reel package includes 9:16 only; extra sizes are quoted separately depending on requirement.
  • Requirements outside package scope may cost extra — new concepts, storylines, or major creative rework count as a new order.
  • Client delays affect delivery timelines — turnaround clocks reset once missing assets/details arrive.
  • AI-generated visuals are subject to platform/tool limitations — always set client expectations accordingly.
  • Execution may be adapted for feasibility — final creative direction can shift based on platform policy or technical constraints.
  • All prices exclude GST — confirm the GST-inclusive total before the client pays.
