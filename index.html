General terms — apply across every service & package
  • ₹10,000 minimum order. Mix and match any services or tiers to reach it — the calculator shows your full order total.
  • Turnaround is per creative. Bulk orders scale with quantity, creative type, tier, and complexity — not the single-creative number shown.
  • Clock starts on handoff, once we've received everything we need — assets and details — not your payment date.
  • New concepts mean a new quote. Major creative rework or extras beyond what's included are quoted separately.
  • AI output can vary slightly between runs — we always match your brief as closely as possible.
  • GST is optional at checkout: included by default at 18% on the order total after any discount, or switch it off in the calculator for a non-GST invoice. Prices elsewhere on the page are pre-GST.
  • One discount, whole order — it applies to the combined total, not to each creative type individually, as either a percentage or a flat ₹ amount.
